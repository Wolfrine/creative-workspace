# Automated product acceptance cases

All cases below: NOT RUN against Noveliaa. These are procedures for future browser/emulator/API adapters, not claims of existing endpoints. P0 blocks the relevant gate. Sources S12–S26 refer to the [research registry](../docs/research/sources.md).

## Fixed test material and environment

Use two independent universes with duplicate document names; 150 notes and 50 scenes per universe; 100,000 manuscript words in total, one 20,000-word scene, and a separate 2 MiB import. Include untitled fragments, CRLF, blank lines, curly punctuation, combining accents, emoji, Hindi/English text, repeated passage quotes, a rejected proposal, an unknown-origin transcript and one missing asset. Put distinct canary strings in ungranted notes, discussions and media metadata.

Record fixture byte hashes outside the app before input. Use separate author, AI-client, peer-A, peer-B and unauthorized identities. Capture editor state before mutation and independent backend/readback afterwards. Two devices means independent persistent stores, not two pages sharing one browser profile.

Browser matrix: current stable desktop Chrome/Firefox/Safari where supported; 320, 390, 768 and 1440 CSS-pixel widths. Confirm physical Android Chrome and iPhone Safari separately in H04. Record exact versions at execution. Proposed performance profile: 4 CPU cores/8 GB desktop; a named midrange physical phone; 10 Mbps down/1 Mbps up, 100 ms RTT for online timing, plus fully offline tests. Report actual hardware and any emulation multiplier.

The dependency-free checker covers structural evidence for A06–A09, A15, A17–A18, A21 and A27. It does not execute the actions, validate a database's permissions, verify a person's identity or prove complete telemetry absence. A12/A13 must exercise both Firebase Rules and server/Admin paths; passing Rules tests alone is insufficient.
