# Human evaluations

All sessions: NOT RUN. These evaluate experience and meaning that scripts cannot settle. Keep task completion, visual judgment, literary fidelity and author acceptance as separate verdicts.

## Session protocol

Author: one 30-minute real writing session, then a separate recovery/review session. Peers: 3–5 willing non-experts, individually, about 15 minutes each. Include the author's own phone, a physical Android phone and iPhone where available; record the actual coverage and mark missing required devices BLOCKED.

Use a synthetic practice scene first. Do not explain where the controls are during a timed task. Count interventions as failures of unassisted completion. Record task time, actions, observed error, participant's own words and evidence; ask recording consent separately. Avoid “did you love it?” and questions that imply an expected emotion. Counterbalance order when comparing variants. Small friendly samples find problems; they do not establish population preferences.

| ID | Task | Pass/fail rule and required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H01 Writing freedom | Starting from the workspace, capture an untitled thought, continue it into prose, add an optional link, then ignore organization for 20 minutes. Compare a matched short session in the author's familiar editor. | Author starts within two actions; zero metadata interruptions or lost words; no forced AI/review step. Author rates interruption burden ≤2/5 and would continue a real draft here. Retain task video/notes and exact stated reasons; high word count is not proof of quality. |
| H02 Orientation | With same-name scenes and 150 notes, find the last paragraph, a specific lore note and its originating discussion; return to the draft with caret intact. | At least 4/5 seeded retrieval tasks completed within 20 s each, no moderator help or universe confusion. Compare Atlas with search/list using equal content. If Atlas adds no retrieval benefit, keep it optional. Retain task times, wrong paths and participant explanation. |
| H03 Recovery confidence | Delete a paragraph, find a prior revision, preview/compare it and restore; explain what “Saved on device” and “Synced” mean while offline. | Exact paragraph recovered within 60 s without help; intervening revision still available. Author correctly explains device versus cloud state and can identify unsynced work. Retain screen recording, hashes and explanation. |
| H04 Physical mobile writing | Type two paragraphs with the software keyboard open; use Hindi IME, autocorrect, selection/formatting, undo, rotate, background and resume; capture one-handed. | Zero lost/duplicated composed characters; caret, current line and save/recovery action remain reachable; no focus jumps or repeated accidental neighboring taps. Primary touch actions target 44×44 CSS px; document any exception. Retain actual-device video/version, not only emulated screenshots. |
| H05 Author control and voice | Compare source, AI proposal and accepted revision; distinguish recovered original, reconstruction, mixed transcript and new writing. Ask the AI for two alternatives to an ambiguous note. | Author can locate exact source/diff, reject without losing either version, and identify what was inferred. Zero unsupported additions presented as source facts; zero silent canon changes. Only the author judges whether intent/voice survive; retain passage-level reasons, not a generic model score. |
| H06 Presentation fidelity | Author checks an edition beside the approved material, with and without illustrations/motion. | All prose/sequence changes and every asset are understood and explicitly approved; none invents unnoticed setting, character or lore. Author can approve a text-only coherent scene. Keep edition manifest and a signed/identified author verdict; an AI's approval is insufficient. |
| H07 Visual quality | Review actual desktop/mobile writing, Atlas/list, review and reader states, including long content and errors. Use the rubric below before scores. | All critical conditions met; critic and lead independently ≥8/10 overall and per critical dimension under Central's future implementation gate; separate QA passes; author explicitly accepts exact candidate. Retain unframed renders, independent reasons and candidate IDs. This review provides no such score. |
| H08 Accessible reading | Read 8–10 minutes; alter type/spacing/theme, use screen-reader/keyboard navigation, block media, enable reduced motion and return after interruption. | No blocked content or unwanted sound; reading position recoverable; reader rates comfort ≥4/5 and names no unresolved reading blocker. Record actual assistive technology/device coverage and issues. Automated accessibility scans cannot substitute. |
| H09 Effortless independent feedback | Peer reads privately, optionally marks a confusing/memorable moment and submits or skips a short reaction. Never show other peer reactions first. | Each peer can finish and leave a voluntary response within 15 s of deciding to respond, without help; skipping is equally possible. No recurring forced prompts interrupt prose. Retain task timing and reader's own wording separately from author interpretation. |
| H10 Respectful learning | Peer explains link access/forwarding and consent; declines behavior and still reads/reacts. Author examines a pause, a direct response and an in-person observation. | Peer understands that link possession may grant access and optional behavior can be declined/withdrawn. Author can distinguish the three evidence types and does not infer a definite emotion from pause data. Retain teach-back notes, consent choice and versioned feedback. |

## Critical visual rubric

Declare pass conditions before scoring a built candidate. A score is reasoned human/critic judgment, not a scientific beauty measurement.

| Dimension | Observable condition |
|---|---|
| Writing hierarchy | Manuscript is the primary working object; title, caret and save state are identifiable; connections/review can be dismissed without abandoning the draft. Long real paragraphs remain readable at all required widths. |
| Typography and density | Comfortable prose measure and spacing with user adjustment; no clipped paragraphs, illegible pale labels or four-column compression on a phone; contrast checks meet the relevant criteria. |
| Ownership and state clarity | Source/proposal/accepted/published distinctions are understandable without relying on color alone. Conflict and offline states explain the next action without taking over ordinary writing. |
| Spatial usefulness | Atlas connections represent real links and retain labels at useful zoom; equivalent list/card actions exist. No decorative graph implies unsupported lore relationships. |
| Reading restraint | Prose remains complete with effects/media off; no forced pace or reaction; reduced motion and interruption/resume work. |

Central's implementation review requires distinct designer, independent critic and lead roles, 5–10 substantive cycles after a credible direction, and lead inspections after rounds 3/5 and final. Those apply after implementation approval, not as a reason to build during research. Preserve the preferred Living Atlas direction unless actual task evidence or the author reopens it. Missing independent review remains an explicit missing gate; repeating scores on unchanged screenshots adds no evidence.

## Comparing richer reading fairly

Use two similarly long, author-approved excerpts; one text-first, one with restrained media. Alternate excerpt/format order across peers to reduce order and familiarity effects. Ask what they remember, where they became uncertain, whether controls interrupted and which presentation they would choose. Keep reasons and disagreements. Do not call completion time, tap count, positive ratings from friends or a small difference proof of immersion or literary superiority.
